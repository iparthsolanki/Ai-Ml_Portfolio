Two agentic GenAI systems and two deployed ML models. Every metric below comes from my own training and evaluation runs.

01 Agentic RAG · LangGraph

Amazon Employee Policy Assistant

An agentic RAG workflow in LangGraph where an LLM classifier routes each query across six paths using conditional edges. Five FAISS retrievers (chunk size 500, overlap 100, top-3 search) feed grounded prompts that keep answers tied to the source documents and reduce hallucination.

  • 6routing paths
  • 5FAISS retrievers
  • Top-3chunk retrieval

02 Agentic AI · LangGraph

AI Resume Analyzer and Interview Coach

A seven-node LangGraph agent that parses a resume, recommends three to five roles with match scores and generates five tailored interview questions. Pydantic structured output keeps every LLM response typed, and an LLM-as-a-judge step scores answers from 0 to 10 with feedback and sample answers.

  • 7graph nodes
  • 3–5role matches
  • 0–10judge scoring

03 NLP · Deep Learning

Banking77 Intent Prediction

A bidirectional GRU (BiGRU) network that classifies customer banking queries into 77 intents across 10,003 samples. Class weights, dropout and early stopping improve generalisation, and the model is served live through FastAPI on Render.

E-Commerce Customer Churn Prediction

Benchmarked six algorithms to predict customer churn, then tuned XGBoost with RandomizedSearchCV and five-fold cross-validation. The final model reached 94.4% precision and 93.5% recall, and is deployed with FastAPI on Render.
